Can your company deliver a European project?

A European project can open the door to partners, markets and institutions in other countries. Fifteen yes-or-no questions tell you whether your company could lead and deliver one: the project and its strategy, the partners, the governance of the collaboration, relations with institutions and the money. It is a preliminary check, to do before you go looking for a call.

Why it matters. On 4 November 2026 the European Commission presented its first EU Sustainable Tourism Strategy: tourism directly contributes 4.1 % of the EU's gross value added and benefits from 15 EU funding programmes. To make the most of that, your company has to be able to lead and deliver the project. See the source.

Why would I give you my data?

Because it is still yours. We only process it on your instructions, under contract. We ask for figures, not names of people or clients. The AI we use does not learn from your data. You can see who has looked at your information and when. With one click, only you can see it. And you can delete all of it with a button. We never use what we see to sell you anything without telling you.

What does it give me that my own reports do not?

It does not replace them: it reads them. Your tools each show you their own part, accounts on one side and sales or bookings on the other. Here they come together in one place and are compared with what happened before and with companies in your sector, based on published accounts of companies similar to yours. It also looks at what the usual reports do not: whether you have gone a year without touching prices, how much your two largest clients weigh, how much of what your company bills goes through you, or which service only makes money if you do not count your own hours. And it is read by someone with experience across many companies, who knows that the same figure does not mean the same in a restaurant as in a hotel.

Why these thresholds Assumption. With three questions per block, one no is already a missing piece, and two noes leave the block without support. They are Evolution Makers’ own judgement and we will adjust them with the results of people who take the check.

Why the weakest block Assumption. Your first step comes from the block with the fewest yeses, because it is the one that would hold the project back first. If there is a tie, the one that comes first goes first: without a project and a strategy, nothing else holds.

What we recommend next Assumption. If two or more of the first four blocks (project, partners, governance of the collaboration and institutions) come out red, the project currently depends on how it is led and delivered, and we mark ‘Talk for 30 minutes’. With one or none, your first step and repeating the check before each call are enough, and we mark the extended version in Evolution Club. If Funding comes out red, we also recommend a partner who specialises in EU funds. This is Evolution Makers’ own judgement.

Your overall result Assumption. No block in red: you have the groundwork to deliver it. Some red: you can, if you close it first. Two or more reds across blocks 1 to 4: right now the project would depend on what you do not have yet.

What it is based on

4.1 %

Tourism in the EU. In 2025 tourism directly contributed 4.1 % of the EU’s gross value added. It employs more than 20 million people in around 5 million companies, and benefits from 15 EU funding programmes, including Cohesion Policy, Interreg and the EMFAF.

European Commission, ‘EU Sustainable Tourism Strategy’, COM(2026) 777 final, 4 November 2026. Checked on 16/11/2026.

53 %

The sea is mostly tourism. In 2022 coastal tourism accounted for 53 % of employment in the EU blue economy and 33 % of its gross value added. The blue economy directly employed close to 4.82 million people, and Spain provides 20 % of those jobs, more than any other country.

European Commission, ‘The EU Blue Economy Report 2025’, executive summary. Checked on 16/11/2026.

Frequently asked questions

Does it tell me whether I will get funding?
No. It is a preliminary check: it tells you whether your company is in a position to lead and deliver a European project. It does not determine whether you are entitled to any funding. The conditions of each programme are set out in the rules of its call.
Why does it focus on leading and delivering?
Because that is what your company has to sustain for the whole project: the strategy, the governance of cooperation between partners, the team and institutional and corporate relations with other countries. For the funds and grants side, the usual approach is to work with a partner who specialises in EU funds: if Funding comes out red and you need one, we will put you in touch.
Why does ‘I don’t know’ count as no?
Because whatever you don’t know before you start, you run into halfway through the project, with partners waiting and deadlines looming. If you don’t know who decides when two partners disagree, you will find out at the worst possible moment.
Is it useful if my company is not in tourism or the sea?
The questions work for any European cooperation project. The sources and the possible routes are chosen for tourism and the blue economy: hotels, travel, restaurants, nautical activities, ports, fishing and the maritime economy.
What happens to my answers?
They are calculated in your browser. There is no server and no sign-up. They only leave your browser if you decide to send them to us: when you book your conversation, if you tick the box ‘Send my result to Evolution Makers to prepare the conversation’, or when you save it in Evolution Club. Your saved link carries your answers: anyone who opens it can see them.
